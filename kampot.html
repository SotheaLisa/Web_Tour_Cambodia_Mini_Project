Welcome to Kampot!


Kampot, is a charming province in southern Cambodia, known for its stunning landscapes, rich culture, and vibrant local life. From the serene riverside to the majestic Bokor Mountain, Kampot offers a unique blend of natural beauty and cultural heritage that captivates every traveler. Whether you're exploring the bustling markets, savoring the famous Kampot pepper, or relaxing by the tranquil beaches, Kampot promises an unforgettable experience filled with warmth and wonder.
